Real animal sound recordings used in `sounds/`:

- `lion-roar.mp3`, `dog-bark.mp3`, `cat-meow.mp3`, `elephant-trumpet.mp3`,
  `bird-chirp.mp3` — from [bradleymatera/AnimalSounds](https://github.com/bradleymatera/AnimalSounds),

- `chicken-cluck.mp3`, `cow-moo.mp3`, `horse-trot.mp3`, `pig-oink.mp3`,
  `sheep-baa.mp3` — from [DJWoodZ/Animal-Sounds](https://github.com/DJWoodZ/Animal-Sounds),

For animals without a dedicated recording, the browser's built-in
text-to-speech speaks a fun sound word instead (see `ANIMAL_SOUNDS` in
`script.js`).
